About MedPoint Partners

Healthcare finance and revenue cycle recruiting — powered by NorthPoint and headquartered in Atlanta.

Our Story

Everyone Wins When Clinicians Can Focus on Patients

We specialize in placing top-tier healthcare finance professionals. We help hospitals and healthcare organizations find and hire the finance people they need, so they can focus on their patients — and that makes everyone win.

Healthcare finance is its own discipline. Payer mix, cost reports, charge capture, denials, coding compliance, and EHR conversions don't show up on a generic accounting résumé. When you call us, you're speaking with someone who knows the difference between a PFS manager and a revenue integrity lead — and which candidates belong in each seat.

Our reach is wide: more than 3 million résumés and a private network of over 15 million profiles. We cast a wide net with targeted advertising and direct sourcing — then narrow it to the few people who fit your role, your systems, and your team.

Sample: Screening Questionnaire

Director of Revenue Cycle — your questions, answered before the first call

We build every questionnaire with your input. For a revenue cycle leader, it might look like this — and every résumé arrives with the candidate's answers.

  • Which EHR and practice-management systems have you run the revenue cycle on — and did you lead a conversion?
  • Where were days in A/R and the denial rate when you started, and where did you take them?
  • How many coders, billers, and PFS staff reported to you, across how many sites?
  • Which payer mix have you managed — Medicare, Medicaid, commercial, workers' comp?
Our Values

What Guides Every Search

The right finance hire protects margins, cash flow, and compliance — and gives clinical teams room to do their work.

  • Patients first. Strong finance and revenue cycle teams keep care organizations healthy enough to serve their communities.
  • Depth over volume. A few right-fit candidates beat a stack of maybe-fits every time.
  • Discretion. Confidential searches and blinded profiles protect clients and candidates alike.
  • Straight answers. Honest feedback on the market, compensation, and candidates — even when it's not what you hoped to hear.
