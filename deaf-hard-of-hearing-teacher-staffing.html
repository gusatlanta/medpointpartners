Deaf & Hard of Hearing Teachers

What We Cover

Deaf and hard of hearing students need a teacher who knows how they access language. We staff DHH teachers on contract for the school year or semester, onsite or remote.

  • Itinerant caseloads across campuses
  • Language, literacy and self-advocacy instruction
  • Hearing technology checks with your audiologist
  • Consultation with general education teachers on access
  • State certificate with a deaf and hard of hearing endorsement checked before a candidate is sent

Common Questions

What Districts Ask Us

Itinerant or classroom — which do you staff?
Both. Itinerant DHH teachers travel to students in their home schools; classroom DHH teachers lead a program on one campus. Tell us which you need.
Can a DHH teacher work remotely?
For many itinerant students, yes — part of the service by video, part in person. See our remote DHH teacher page.
How fast can you send candidates?
We send a blinded shortlist of screened candidates within 48 to 72 hours of your request — credentials checked and your screening questions answered.
How does contract staffing work?
Your clinician or teacher works on MedPoint's payroll for the school year, the semester or a set number of weeks. We handle weekly payroll, taxes and workers' comp, and you get one invoice at the agreed rate. If you want to keep them, contract-to-hire and direct hire are available.
What does a blinded candidate mean?
A blinded profile shows a candidate's credentials, experience and location — but no name, employer or school. When you ask for the introduction, we set it up.
