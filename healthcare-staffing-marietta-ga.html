RNs, LPNs, CNAs, medical assistants and patient access staff for Cobb County's main hospital campus and the practices that surround it.

Cobb County

Healthcare Hiring in Marietta

Marietta is Cobb County's healthcare hub. A large hospital campus anchors the city, and around it sit cardiology, oncology, orthopedic and primary care practices, plus imaging centers and a growing number of post-acute and senior living communities in east and west Cobb.

Nursing coverage is the steadiest request: LPNs and CNAs for senior living and post-acute care, RNs for clinics and procedure areas, and float MAs for multi-site practices that move staff between Marietta, Kennesaw and Acworth. We also staff front desk and patient access teams for practices that cannot keep their phones answered during flu season.

Screening in Marietta
  • Multi-site groups want staff willing to float between locations
  • Senior living roles often need weekend availability
  • Flu season brings front desk and MA surges every year
  • Many candidates live in Kennesaw, Acworth and west Cobb
