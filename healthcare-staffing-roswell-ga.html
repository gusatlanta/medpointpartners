Home health nurses, physical therapy staff, pediatric and dental assistants for Roswell's community hospital, clinics and home care agencies.

Fulton County

Healthcare Hiring in Roswell

Roswell is a family-heavy suburb with a community hospital, a lot of pediatric, dental and primary care practices, and a growing home health and hospice sector serving North Fulton's older residents. Outpatient physical therapy clinics line Holcomb Bridge Road and Highway 9.

Home health agencies need RNs and LPNs who are comfortable driving between patient homes and charting on a tablet. PT clinics need PTAs, aides and front desk staff who can keep a full schedule moving. Pediatric and dental offices need assistants who are good with kids and parents. We screen for each of those settings rather than treating them as one pool.

Screening in Roswell
  • Home health roles need a reliable car and mileage tracking
  • Point-of-care charting experience is asked up front
  • PT clinics often need early-morning and evening coverage
  • Many candidates live in Roswell, Milton and east Cobb
