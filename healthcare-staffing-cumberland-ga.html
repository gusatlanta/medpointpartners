Healthcare Hiring in Cumberland

Cumberland's healthcare jobs are as much in office towers as in clinics. Health insurers, benefits administrators, revenue cycle firms and medical management companies keep back-office teams near the I-75 and I-285 interchange, while urgent care, imaging and orthopedic clinics serve the dense residential neighborhoods around it.

The back-office side needs coders, utilization review and prior authorization specialists, and claims and A/R staff, often hybrid. The clinic side needs radiologic techs, urgent care MAs and X-ray-trained staff who can work evenings and weekends. We staff both, and candidates from Smyrna, Vinings and east Cobb have short commutes.

Screening in Cumberland
  • Back-office roles are often hybrid; urgent care roles are onsite
  • Evening and weekend shifts are normal in urgent care
  • Payer-side experience is screened separately from provider-side
  • Smyrna, Vinings and east Cobb candidates have short commutes
