Why Build Your Book in School Staffing

Required by Law. Publicly Funded. About as Recession-Proof as Staffing Gets.

Districts don't stop needing speech therapists in a downturn. Federal law (IDEA) requires the services in every student's IEP, and public education budgets pay for them — every school year.

And the work is mostly contract, which means districts come back every semester and every fall. Build relationships once; they keep ordering.
