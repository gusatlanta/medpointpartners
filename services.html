Contract-to-Hire

Try Before You Hire

Start a candidate on contract and convert them to a full-time employee when you're confident in the fit. It reduces risk for both sides — especially useful while headcount approval is in progress.

  • Typical trial period of 90–180 days
  • Candidate works on your team from day one
  • Conversion fee structure agreed upfront
  • Ideal when headcount approval is in progress
  • Candidate learns your systems and payers before converting
Explore Contract-to-Hire

Best For

  • Roles where culture fit is critical
  • New positions with evolving scope
  • Business office and PFS teams
  • Organizations with a hiring freeze in process
  • High-volume coding and billing teams
Fee Structure
Bill rate on contract; reduced conversion fee upon permanent hire

Fully Customized Recruiting & Staffing Solutions

No two healthcare organizations hire the same way. We build a recruiting program around your timeline, budget, and team structure — from single hires to full department builds.

Sample: Screening Questionnaire

Director of Revenue Cycle — your questions, answered before the first call

We build every questionnaire with your input. For a revenue cycle leader, it might look like this — and every résumé arrives with the candidate's answers.

  • Which EHR and practice-management systems have you run the revenue cycle on — and did you lead a conversion?
  • Where were days in A/R and the denial rate when you started, and where did you take them?
  • How many coders, billers, and PFS staff reported to you, across how many sites?
  • Which payer mix have you managed — Medicare, Medicaid, commercial, workers' comp?
