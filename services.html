Temp-to-Perm

Temp-to-Perm

Start someone as a temp and move them to your payroll when you're sure of the fit. It lowers the risk for both sides — and works well while headcount approval is in progress.

  • Typical temp period of 90–180 days
  • They work on your team from day one
  • Conversion fee agreed upfront
  • Ideal when headcount approval is in progress
  • They learn your clinic, systems, and patients before converting
Explore Temp-to-Perm

Best For

  • Roles where team fit is critical
  • New positions with evolving scope
  • Front office and patient access teams
  • Clinics under a hiring freeze
  • High-volume medical assistant and nursing teams
Fee Structure
Bill rate as a temp; reduced conversion fee when you hire them

Fully Customized Recruiting & Staffing Solutions

No two healthcare organizations hire the same way. We build a recruiting program around your timeline, budget, and team structure — from single hires to full department builds.

Sample: Screening Questionnaire

Orthopedic Physician Assistant (PA-C) — your questions, answered before the first call

We build every questionnaire with your input. For an ortho PA, it might look like this — and every résumé arrives with the candidate's answers.

  • Which orthopedic areas have you worked in — spine, joints, sports medicine, hand, trauma?
  • Do you first-assist in the OR? About how many cases a week?
  • How many patients a day do you see in clinic on your own?
  • Is your state license active, and do you hold a DEA registration?
