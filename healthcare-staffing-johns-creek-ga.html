Healthcare Staffing in Johns Creek, GA

Clinic nurses, ophthalmic and specialty MAs, lab and phlebotomy staff and life sciences support for Johns Creek's hospital, practices and Technology Park.

Fulton County

Healthcare Hiring in Johns Creek

Johns Creek has a community hospital, a dense cluster of specialty practices along Medlock Bridge Road (GA-141) and State Bridge Road, and life sciences and medical device companies in Technology Park. Ophthalmology, GI, dermatology and pediatric practices are all well represented.

Specialty practices here need MAs with specialty skills: ophthalmic technicians who can work up a patient, GI techs, and derm MAs who can assist with procedures. Labs and diagnostics companies need phlebotomists and lab assistants. The city has no rail transit, so we check commute fit early, and many candidates come from Duluth, Suwanee and Alpharetta.

Screening in Johns Creek
  • Ophthalmic certifications (COA, COT) are confirmed when you ask for them
  • Phlebotomy roles may need early-morning starts
  • No rail transit, so commute is part of screening
  • Candidates often come from Duluth, Suwanee and Alpharetta
