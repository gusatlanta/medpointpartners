Healthcare Hiring in Norcross

Norcross and the Jimmy Carter Boulevard and Buford Highway corridor are home to community health centers, family medicine and pediatric clinics, and urgent cares serving a large Spanish-speaking population. Next door, Technology Parkway and Peachtree Corners hold medical device, diagnostics and health tech companies.

Community clinics need bilingual medical assistants, patient navigators and front desk staff who can handle high patient volume and sliding-fee programs. Device and diagnostics companies need customer support, order processing and lab support staff with healthcare backgrounds. We recruit for both, and candidates from Lilburn, Tucker and Peachtree Corners have short commutes.

Screening in Norcross

  • Community health centers often use sliding-fee and grant reporting rules
