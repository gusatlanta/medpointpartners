Healthcare Staffing in Duluth, GA

Bilingual medical assistants, nurses, front desk and coding staff for Duluth's hospital campus and Gwinnett's multilingual practices.

Gwinnett County

Healthcare Hiring in Duluth

Duluth has a hospital campus and a dense mix of primary care, internal medicine, OB-GYN and dental practices serving a very diverse county. Many of those practices serve patients who prefer Korean, Spanish, Vietnamese or Chinese, and they hire with that in mind.

Language skill is a real requirement here, not a bonus. We place bilingual medical assistants, front desk staff and patient coordinators and confirm their language ability before you interview. Practices along Pleasant Hill Road and Satellite Boulevard also need coders and billers who understand the payer mix in Gwinnett, including Medicaid managed care plans.

Screening in Duluth
  • Language ability is confirmed before interviews
  • Medicaid managed care experience is useful for billing roles
  • Evening and Saturday clinic hours are common
  • Candidates often live in Duluth, Suwanee, Lawrenceville and Johns Creek
