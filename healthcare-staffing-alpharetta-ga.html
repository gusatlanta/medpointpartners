Medical assistants, clinic nurses, patient access staff and certified coders for the specialty practices, surgery centers and health IT companies along GA-400.

Fulton County

Healthcare Hiring in Alpharetta

Alpharetta's healthcare employers are mostly outpatient. Orthopedic, dermatology, cardiology and women's health practices fill the medical office buildings off Windward Parkway, North Point and Old Milton, and ambulatory surgery centers sit beside them. Health IT and revenue cycle companies round out the market, which is why coders and billing specialists are as easy to find here as clinicians are hard to keep.

A practice here usually needs someone who can start fast and fit a small team: a medical assistant who can room patients for two providers, a front desk lead who knows referrals and prior authorizations, or an LPN who can cover triage calls while a nurse is on leave. MedPoint works from the same North Fulton corridor, so candidates are screened for the GA-400 commute before you see them.

Screening in Alpharetta
  • Specialty experience matters: ortho, derm and cardiology MAs are screened separately
  • EHR familiarity (Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks and others) is asked up front
  • Hybrid coding roles are common; we confirm home-office setup
  • Surgery centers often need early start times, so availability is checked
