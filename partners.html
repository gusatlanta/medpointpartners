Join Our Partner Program

You spent your career building relationships in healthcare. Put them to work — you bring the hiring need, we do all the recruiting, and you get paid.

For Physicians, Healthcare Leaders & Anyone Whose Network Hires

You get the order. We fill it.

You get paid for every hour someone works.

You get the order

Hear "we need to hire" from someone in your network — and make the introduction.

We fill it

Our recruiters run the entire search — sourcing, screening, and placing. Temp, temp-to-perm, or direct hire.

You get paid

A referral fee on direct hire placements, and a recurring share of every hour your client's temps work — for the life of the assignment.

What You Earn

Simple, generous, recurring.

10%

Direct hire placements

Your client hires direct through us — you earn 10% of the placement fee we collect. Paid when we collect from the client.

25%

Temp net spread

Your client runs temps or temp-to-perm workers — you earn 25% of the net spread on every hour they bill.

Recurring

While they work

Temp commissions aren't one-time. They keep paying as long as your client's temps keep billing. Build a book.

15th

Paid every month

Commissions are calculated on money actually received and paid on the 15th of the following month. Every dollar tracked for you.

Who This Is For

Anyone whose network hires.

Physicians and practice owners. Nurse leaders and practice administrators. Hospital executives. Healthcare consultants, CPAs, and vendors. Retired healthcare executives. You never become a recruiter — you bring the hiring need and stay the relationship owner; our team fills the order.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

  1. Apply below — we review every partner personally and set you up.
  2. Hear "we need two medical assistants" (or a nurse manager, a PA, a whole front office) — send it in.
  3. Our recruiters fill the order — temp, temp-to-perm, or direct hire.
  4. You're paid on the 15th of the following month — 10% of the fee on direct hire, 25% of net spread on temps, recurring while the worker bills.

Net spread = what the client pays, minus worker wages and employer burden (taxes, workers comp, benefits, compliance). Commissions are calculated on payments actually received; refunded or unpaid invoices are reversed. Self-referrals and organizations already under agreement with us don't qualify. Earned commissions are never reduced.
