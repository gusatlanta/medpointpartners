Special Education in Georgia

How Georgia Districts Staff IEP Services

The Georgia Department of Education (GaDOE) oversees special education through its Special Education Services and Supports division. Educator certification runs through the Georgia Professional Standards Commission (GaPSC), and clinical licenses through the state’s professional licensing boards.

Every Georgia school has a Student Support Team (SST) that works on learning and behavior concerns, often before a special education referral. Once a student qualifies, the IEP services are required under IDEA and publicly funded. We staff them on contract for the school year or the semester first, by teletherapy where it fits, with direct hire available. MedPoint is based in Atlanta, so Georgia is home.

RESAs, GNETS and GLRS

Georgia’s 16 Regional Educational Service Agencies (RESAs) serve districts by region, and some act as fiscal agents for state special education programs. The Georgia Network for Educational and Therapeutic Support (GNETS) runs regional programs for students with severe emotional and behavioral disorders, and the Georgia Learning Resources System (GLRS) provides training and support. Contract staff can be placed with your district, or with the RESA or district that runs a regional program.

Credentials We Check in Georgia

Licensed for Georgia Students

Every candidate's Georgia license or credential is checked before we send them — onsite or by teletherapy.

Speech-Language Pathologists

School SLPs hold a GaPSC service certificate in Speech and Language Pathology (P-12). Clinical licensure comes from the Secretary of State’s Speech-Language Pathology, Audiology, and Hearing Aid Dispensing Board, and a valid Georgia license or the ASHA CCC can count toward the certificate.

School Psychologists

School psychologists hold a GaPSC School Psychology service certificate at the specialist (Level 6) level or higher, which requires a state-approved program and the GACE school psychology assessment.

School Social Workers

School social workers hold a GaPSC School Social Work service certificate.

Occupational Therapists

School OTs are licensed by the Georgia State Board of Occupational Therapy.

Physical Therapists

School PTs are licensed by the Georgia State Board of Physical Therapy.

Common Questions

What Georgia Districts Ask Us

Is MedPoint based in Georgia?
Yes. MedPoint Partners, powered by NorthPoint, is based in Atlanta. We staff districts across Georgia, onsite or by teletherapy, and we check the GaPSC certificates and state licenses your roles call for before a candidate is sent.
Does a Georgia school SLP need a GaPSC certificate or a state license?
Districts check for the GaPSC Speech and Language Pathology service certificate. The state license is a separate credential, and a valid Georgia license or the ASHA CCC is one route to the certificate. We check whichever your district requires.
Can you staff a GNETS program or a RESA?
Yes. Tell us which agency holds the contract, the sites and the caseload. We place staff with the agency that runs the program, and MedPoint, as employer of record, sends one invoice.
Why does a fully staffed school matter for IDEA compliance?
Under IDEA, every student with an IEP is owed the special education and related services written into it, on the schedule written into it. When a position sits empty, minutes are missed, evaluations slip past their timelines, and districts can owe make-up (compensatory) services or face state complaints. Filling the seat quickly — on contract or by teletherapy — keeps services on schedule.
How fast can you send candidates?
We send a blinded shortlist of screened candidates within 48 to 72 hours of your request — credentials checked and your screening questions answered.
