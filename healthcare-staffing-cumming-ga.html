Forsyth County

Healthcare Hiring in Cumming

Forsyth County has grown fast, and healthcare has followed. A hospital campus in Cumming has expanded alongside new medical office buildings along GA-400 and Highway 20, and primary care, pediatrics, orthopedics and urgent care keep opening locations to serve new subdivisions.

New locations mean hiring several roles at once: front desk, MAs, an X-ray tech and a nurse for a clinic opening, or surgical techs for a new OR suite. Temp staffing bridges the gap while full-time teams are built through Direct Hire. Much of the local talent commutes south on GA-400, so a role in Cumming can be an easy sell to people tired of that drive.
