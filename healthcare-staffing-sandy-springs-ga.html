Healthcare Hiring in Sandy Springs

Sandy Springs holds a dense hospital cluster on the stretch of Peachtree Dunwoody Road known as Pill Hill. Hospitals, physician office towers and surgery centers sit side by side, and the medical groups attached to them need clinical and administrative staff in equal measure.

Surgical and procedural areas drive much of the demand: surgical technologists, OR and PACU nurses, sterile processing and the unit secretaries who keep floors running. Physician groups in the office towers need credentialing and payer enrollment specialists every time they add providers. The Medical Center, Dunwoody and Sandy Springs MARTA stations make shift work practical without a car.
