Healthcare Staffing in Athens, GA

Nurses, allied health staff and remote coding and billing support for Athens and northeast Georgia's regional hospitals and clinics.

Athens-Clarke County

Healthcare Hiring in Athens

Athens is northeast Georgia's healthcare center, about seventy miles from Atlanta. Two regional hospitals, a medical school partnership with the University of Georgia, and specialty practices draw patients from a wide rural area, and the university feeds a steady stream of nursing and allied health graduates.

Distance shapes how we staff Athens. Onsite clinical roles are recruited locally, from Athens-Clarke, Oconee, Jackson and Madison counties. Coding, billing, prior authorization and credentialing can often be done remotely, so practices here can tap remote talent from across Georgia for those seats.

Screening in Athens
  • Onsite roles are recruited locally; back-office roles can be remote
  • New graduates may still be awaiting licensure, so we confirm status
  • Rural patient populations mean wider service areas for home health
  • Oconee, Jackson and Madison county candidates commute in easily
