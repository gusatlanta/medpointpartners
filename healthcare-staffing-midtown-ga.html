Healthcare Staffing in Midtown Atlanta, GA

Hospital and clinic nurses, imaging technologists, clinical research coordinators and patient access staff for Midtown's academic medicine and outpatient clinics.

Fulton County (City of Atlanta)

Healthcare Hiring in Midtown

Midtown combines an academic hospital, a growing cluster of outpatient clinics and cancer care, and research programs tied to the universities around it. Work here is busier and more specialized than in the suburbs: oncology infusion, imaging, research protocols and high-volume patient access.

Those employers often need help for a defined stretch: an imaging tech to cover a vacancy, a research coordinator for the length of a study, or patient access representatives for a new clinic opening. Temp and contract coverage fits that pattern. Arts Center, Midtown and North Avenue MARTA stations put most of the district on transit, which widens the pool for shift work.

Screening in Midtown
  • ARRT and modality registrations are confirmed before submittal
  • Research roles often want GCP training; we ask for it
  • Shift and weekend availability is screened for imaging
  • Arts Center, Midtown and North Avenue MARTA stations serve the district
