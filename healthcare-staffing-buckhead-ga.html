Registered nurses, medical assistants, rehab staff and patient coordinators for the hospitals, specialty practices and aesthetic clinics along Peachtree Road.

Fulton County (City of Atlanta)

Healthcare Hiring in Buckhead

Buckhead's healthcare market runs along Peachtree Road: a major hospital campus, a well-known rehabilitation hospital for spinal cord and brain injuries, and a long line of private specialty practices in between. Plastic surgery, dermatology, aesthetics, fertility and concierge medicine are all well represented, and those practices compete on patient experience.

That makes polish part of the job description. Aesthetic and concierge practices want patient coordinators and MAs who can handle high-touch patients and quote procedures confidently. Rehab settings need therapy aides and nurses who are comfortable with complex mobility needs. We screen for both, and the Buckhead and Lenox MARTA stations help candidates who prefer not to drive into the district.

Screening in Buckhead
  • Elective practices often ask for sales-minded patient coordinators
  • Procedure nurses are screened for sedation and recovery experience
  • Rehab roles may require lifting and transfer experience
  • MARTA access via the Buckhead and Lenox stations widens the pool
